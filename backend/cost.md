💰 Meta WhatsApp Business API Costs
1. Conversation-Based Pricing
Meta charges per 24-hour conversation window (not per message):

Business-Initiated Conversations:

When YOU send the first message to a customer

Cost varies by country:

India: ~$0.014 per conversation

US: ~$0.035 per conversation

UK: ~$0.055 per conversation

UAE: ~$0.045 per conversation

User-Initiated Conversations:

When customer messages you first

Cost varies by country:

India: ~$0.008 per conversation

US: ~$0.020 per conversation

UK: ~$0.030 per conversation

UAE: ~$0.025 per conversation

2. Free Tier
1,000 free user-initiated conversations per month (per phone number)

Resets monthly

Only applies to user-initiated conversations

4. Additional Costs
Template Message Approval:

Free to submit templates

Approval required for business-initiated messages

Phone Number:

Included with WhatsApp Business API

No separate phone number fee

Meta Business Verification:

Free, but required for production

Takes 3-5 business days

5. Cost Optimization Tips
✅ Keep conversations within 24 hours - All messages in same window = 1 charge
✅ Let patients initiate - Cheaper than business-initiated
✅ Use free tier wisely - First 1,000/month are free
✅ Avoid multiple conversations - Complete booking in one session
✅ Send appointment reminders carefully - Each reminder = new conversation if >24hrs

6. Real Example for Your Bot
Scenario: Hospital with 3,000 appointments/month in India
